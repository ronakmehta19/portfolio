Step 1: Set Audacious Goals

Philosophy: If it doesn't scare you, it's too small.

CriteriaThe Standard
ImpactChanges the Game Not just "10% better"
ClarityBinary Outcome Did we do it? Yes/No.
FocusThe "One Thing" Pick 1 major goal, not 5.

Litmus Test Checklist

  • check_box Can I write it on a post-it note?
  • check_box Does the team know why it matters?
  • check_box Directly tied to company revenue/mission?

warning Strategy Traps

block "Peanut Buttering"

Spreading resources too thin across too many projects.

block Set & Forget

Announcing the goal in Jan and never mentioning it again.

block Secret Strategy

Keeping the "Why" in your head. Over-communicate context.

block Ignoring Capacity

Setting goals without checking if the team is burnt out.

  • • Dictates the "How" instead of the "What".
  • • Demands status updates randomly.

  • • Sets clear outcomes, trusts the method.
  • • Asks "What is blocking you?"
  • • Connects people to solve problems.
